Since Graph API access is not available, each staff member adds one Outlook inbox rule to their own account — 2 minutes, self-service — forwarding HiBob emails to the nominated WFM mailbox. One standard flow then watches that single mailbox and handles the whole team.

1
Staff inbox rule (self-service) — each of the 22 ECS team members adds an Outlook rule: "From: no-reply@hibob.com → Forward to: [nominated WFM mailbox]". Takes under 2 minutes, no IT needed, no technical knowledge required
2
Trigger — "When a new email arrives (V3)" standard Outlook connector on the nominated WFM mailbox, filtered to From: no-reply@hibob.com and Subject contains: Invitation:
3
Identify employee — parse the email's original To field (preserved in the forwarded message) to extract firstname.lastname@ecscard.org.uk, then convert to display name using a Power Automate expression
4
Parse — extract leave type and start/end date from the subject line using split/trim expressions. Exactly as the prototype demonstrates
5
Write — create item in AbsenceRequests SharePoint list, Status = Approved, Source = HiBob, SubmittedAt = email received timestamp
6
Notify — post a confirmation to the WFM Teams channel with employee name, dates, and a reminder to update Teams Shifts
